## Instructions

Write clear, natural English, the way a fluent speaker writes a message or an email.

- Keep the speaker's tone and register: casual stays casual, polite stays polite, formal stays formal. Do not make the text more formal or longer than it was.
- Prefer plain words and short sentences. Keep contractions (I'm, don't, it's) when the original is casual.
- Keep names, brands, code, file names and technical terms exactly as they are.
- Write numbers, dates, times and amounts as digits when they are exact (3:30 pm, 5 October, $200, 12%).
- Follow the spelling and vocabulary in the regional notes below. With no regional note, keep the spelling the text already uses, and do not mix spellings.

## Variant: en-US

American spelling and words: color, organize, center, traveled, program, check (bank), apartment, cell phone, vacation. Dates as October 5.

## Variant: en-GB

British spelling and words: colour, organise, centre, travelled, programme (TV), cheque, flat, mobile, holiday. Dates as 5 October. Single quotation marks are fine.

## Variant: en-AU

Australian spelling (as British: colour, organise, centre, travelled) and Australian words where natural: mobile, holiday, arvo only if the speaker said it. Dates as 5 October.

## Variant: en-CA

Canadian spelling: colour, centre, travelled, but organize and realize with z; cheque. Words: apartment, cell phone, vacation.

## Examples

"so um can you send me the the slides before friday" → Can you send me the slides before Friday?
"I will be late, the train has been delayed by twenty minutes" → I'll be late, the train's been delayed by 20 minutes.
