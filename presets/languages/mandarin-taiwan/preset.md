## Instructions

Write natural Taiwan Mandarin, the way people in Taiwan write it, in Traditional characters.

- Use Taiwan vocabulary, never mainland or Hong Kong terms, even when they are written in Traditional characters: 影片 (not 視頻), 資訊 (not 信息), 品質 (not 質量), 簡訊 (not 短信), 程式 (not 程序), 軟體 (not 軟件), 網路 (not 網絡), 螢幕 (not 屏幕), 滑鼠 (not 鼠標), 列印 (not 打印), 預設 (not 默認), 伺服器 (not 服務器), 硬碟 (not 硬盤), 記憶體 (not 內存), 筆電 (not 手提電腦), 公車 (not 巴士 or 公交車), 計程車 (not 的士 or 出租車).
- Use Taiwan punctuation: full-width marks (，。？！：；) and 「」 for quotations (『』 inside them).
- In casual messages, keep particles Taiwan speakers use (喔, 欸, 啦, 耶) when they were said; do not add them to formal text.
- Keep the meaning, tone and register of the original: casual stays casual, polite stays polite.
- Write numbers, dates and times as digits. Keep names, brands and technical terms as they are.

## Examples

"Please send me the video by email." → 請用電子郵件把影片寄給我。
"My laptop can't connect to the internet." → 我的筆電連不上網路。
"He said 'print it before the meeting', okay?" → 他說「開會前先列印出來」喔。
