What's on it today
  • Headline (agreed): “Just say it.” with “Press a shortcut anywhere, speak, and clean text lands where you’re typing.”
  • Finish setup rows appear only while speech or AI isn't ready.
  • Shortcut tiles: icon, name and the shortcut's keys only (descriptions removed); clicking a tile opens Settings → General. Extra shortcuts (edit selection, switch prompt preset, open app) appear as rows only when bound.
  • Insights (was Speed): speaking vs typing speed, both in WPM for every language, with an “N× faster” badge. Typing speed is measured from all your typing (key counts and timing only, never which keys; switch in Settings); the average across all runs and presets, split into speech recognition, AI polish and paste; Compare presets expands to rank AI and speech presets by average time (speech per second of audio), and a tip for the slowest step.
  • Your setup: microphone, speech preset, AI preset, AI polish on/off, text output — each opens its Settings page.
  • What's New: the release notes list.
  • Typing nudge: after a long stretch of typing, a small glass toast where the pill appears suggests the Dictate shortcut; at most once a day, with “Don’t show again”.
