When nothing was said

You start Dictate, say nothing, and press the shortcut again. Today the pill turns red and says “Didn't catch that” for 1.5 s. With the calm fade it shows no text and no red: the label blurs away, the aurora drains to a soft grey glow, and the pill narrows a little and slides away. It takes about 0.8 s, and nothing is pasted, as before.

What changes in the app

Behaviour

  • Only the pill's look changes. No-speech detection (the voice check and the hallucination guard) is unchanged, and nothing is pasted.
  • It applies to Dictate, Translate and Ask, whether you stop with the shortcut or by clicking the pill.
  • Real problems (speech server offline, AI failed, setup needed) keep the red error pill with their message.
  • Esc is unchanged: the pill hides at once. The same fade could be used there later.
  • A new run started during the fade replaces it at once. With reduced motion, the pill just fades.

Implementation sketch

  • Frontend only. The backend already sends pipeline:error with stt_no_speech_detected. The pill turns that into a new quiet state instead of the error pill.
  • quiet shows no content, is 104 pt wide, and hides after about 0.6 s. The aurora gets a quiet mode: the previous glow drains to grey, plus a brief soft grey flash.
  • The onboarding tutorial still gets the event and keeps its own hint.
  • New plan quiet-no-speech, and the lesson in CLAUDE.md is updated.
