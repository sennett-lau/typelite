How it behaves
  • Edits ("make it shorter", "translate this") replace the highlight, like today. No panel: the pill flashes Done.
  • Questions ("what does this mean?") open a panel just above the pill, on the screen you are using. It shows your question (with a hint that it was about the highlight), the answer, and actions: Copy, Insert (at the cursor, or replace the highlight), and ✕.
  • The panel stays until you close it (Esc, ✕, or starting a new run); it never takes focus from your app, so you can keep typing. Long answers scroll inside the panel (max ~190 pt).
  • Couldn't replace (the app blocked it): the same panel shows the result, already on the clipboard, with "Copied" and a retry.
  • Needs live information: the panel says so and offers "Answer anyway", as today's Ask window does.
  • Today these appear in a separate window in the middle of the screen; this design moves them above the pill.
