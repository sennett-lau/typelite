What to look at
  • Onboarding AI step uses the same card, states and sheet as speech. "Built-in" for AI is a proposal: it would download a small open model and run it on this Mac. Skipping still lets dictation work with the raw transcript.
  • The sheet form is the same four fields; "Advanced" hides Extra fields (for thinking models).
  • Settings → AI: "AI polish uses" choice, then Polish (clean-up switch, four style cards, match the app), Translation (language chips), and Advanced (selected text, custom instructions) collapsed.
  • Settings → General: Shortcuts (all five, including Switch language and Cancel), Recording (start/stop style, microphone, level, mute), Output (paste or type).
