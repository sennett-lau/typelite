Dark mode styles

Midnight BlueNavy-tinted glass, a soft blue glow in the corners, brighter blue accent #5EA8FF. Calm and matches the icon.
AuroraNear-black glass lit by teal and violet light from the corners, teal accent #3FD8C2. The most striking of the three.
PlumWarm purple glass with pink and amber glows, pink accent #FF7AB8. Friendly and very unlike other dictation apps.

In every style, each feature gets its own colour (Dictate, Translate, Ask) on its tile icon, switches glow softly, and page titles pick up a hint of the accent. Light mode keeps the current look plus the per-feature colours.
